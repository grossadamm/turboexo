BEFORE SENDCUTSEND
These are source drawings, not validated production cut files.
Separate the chosen profiles and remove mesh/internal lines, center marks,
dimensions, and duplicate geometry. Export each finished part separately.
Confirm units, physical dimensions, and fit against the actual head/gaskets
and turbo housings before ordering. Check wastegate-flapper clearance on the
chosen outlet design. Select plate thickness separately for the cutting order.

SCALE FINDINGS
Head DWG: original metadata says inches, but numeric geometry appears to be
millimeters. Interpreted as mm: approx 347.6 x 83.3 overall, four 48.9 x 30.1
oblong ports, nine 12.7 diameter holes, and 10 modeled thickness.
These are drawing measurements, not independently verified fit dimensions.
Turbo DWG: inspection conversion yielded sub-unit coordinates (inlet opening
approx 0.0452 coordinate units). Meter-scale geometry is plausible, but original
unit interpretation has not been verified. Do not import assuming mm without
checking scale. The inspection conversion omitted metadata; its units are not
proof of the original DWG unit setting. No converted inspection DXFs included.
