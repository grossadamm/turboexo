Data and display notes

576 original samples from 2026-10-23_17.35.10.csv. Numbers use recorded samples; arc and ribbon movement interpolate between samples. Speed, fuel and oil temperature are simulated preview inputs while their data connections are wired. Simulated road speed is independent of the stationary engine recording; it is not inferred from RPM. Boost/vacuum is derived from recorded MAP minus barometric pressure. CLT/IAT use inferred °F units. Oil-pressure calibration and AFR readiness are unverified; the tuning view shows recorded O₂ and target values without treating them as validated mixture. Gauge ranges are illustrative scales, not calibrated safe bands or warning thresholds. RPM scale is not a redline recommendation. This is replay, not live telemetry.
