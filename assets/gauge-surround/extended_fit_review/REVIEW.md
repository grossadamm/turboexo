# Extended vehicle mounting metrology

**Keep the current cutter coordinates for a physical fit check. The photos do not support a release datum shift on either side.** The right slot’s relative shape is well supported. The left slot plane, seat depth and driver path are the higher priorities. The new checks expose two independent problems. A camera fit can score better near the column and predict worse at the outer tabs. Even a fixed overview camera leaves a metric depth ambiguity if the close-up has no reliable connection to a known 3D feature.

## 1. Independent old-to-new registration

I decoded the three original HEICs at 4032 × 3024 and matched their initial direct hashes against the archived originals. IMG_2850 is the overview, IMG_2851 is car right and IMG_2852 is car left. The exact STEP and STL hashes are in [source_hashes.txt](evidence/source_hashes.txt). The original-grid reference is the existing 6.35 mm reconstruction calibration (216.278 mm/world unit in the registered frame). I used the actual RealityKit poses with the existing 3302/3276 px video pinhole lenses and corrected still focal. New graph paper, wiring, stalks, the wheel, both openings and tape were excluded from the registration. Candidate feature matching used the original overview pose only as a broad search gate. That affects discovery coverage. The fit used new independently reconstructed landmarks.

The full-resolution and downsampled descriptor searches yielded 24 spatially distinct tracks after old-view filtering. Each required three or more old observations, at least 5° viewing separation and agreement across old cameras. Twenty-one occupy the column recess, chiefly the NC11 label and nearby pieces. Three are on the outer shroud. Candidate masks also covered green steel and left chassis, but none survived as usable old-to-new 3D tracks. Older side views show the stable carrier and attachment hardware. They show why the mock-up front rim cannot be recovered directly: tape hides the target tabs and openings while the visible oval in the lower web is a different feature. The manually identified points were treated as diagnostics. For example, the two most repeatable label picks triangulate with 3.6 px RMS in old images but miss the released new projection by 26 and 33 px. The far lip clip and cast hole are weaker. A manual-only refit makes outer extrapolation unstable when a single feature is removed. The data leave landmark-definition, old camera error and relative motion of parts of the column/shroud as possible causes. They supply no validated global pose correction. **Effect at the existing depth.** The nominal texture pose maps the outer centers to R **(137.50, −26.20, −110.67) mm** and L **(137.85, 179.61, −107.47) mm**. Against the released slotted STEP these are shifts of 2.24 and 6.30 mm in pad-plane magnitude, respectively. The mostly upward shift differs from the earlier alternate shroud pick, especially on the left. These coordinates are retained as a falsifiable registration case, without averaging them into the CAD. In pad coordinates the displacement is R (−0.89, −2.07) and L (+0.30, −6.29) mm. Positive major is car-left and positive minor is approximately hood-down. The three left/right candidate views cannot all be made authoritative by a single offset.

The new EXIF tags are 48 mm equivalent for the overview and 50 mm for the macros. The actual optical focal tags are 6.765 and 2.22 mm, and the two macros have different digital-zoom tags. I used 5590/5820 px as central effective pinhole focal hypotheses. Re-solving the texture pose from 5100 to 6200 px changes the fixed-plane estimate by about 3.9 mm right and 4.9 mm left between endpoints. Principal point ±65 px horizontally/∓45 px vertically and radial k1 ±0.18 also move the extrapolated centers. Re-triangulating the same old tracks at ±1% focal changes the texture fit and inferred centers by up to about 2 mm. Substituting the historical 205.822 scale with rotation and origin held fixed changes the new inferred center by 2.3 mm right and 8.3 mm left. That is an alternative calibration sensitivity. The historical frame is superseded by the corrected grid registration. No new paper-to-metal scale was used. ## 2. New stereo checks on metal and shroud

- I extracted five straight steel edge segments per side in each view using local Lab color-gradient profiles. Virtual intersections are built from the straight portions, away from the rounded corners. Four of the five point anchors are new, with one earlier finger apex for leverage. I compared that registration to the earlier five-anchor fit, withholding both the slot rim and inner round hole in each. The outlines use the near steel lip where it is identifiable. A rolled silhouette, glare or the notch wires can perturb an edge pick. [Right close edge trace](evidence/lines_right_close.jpg) · [Left close edge trace](evidence/lines_left_close.jpg) · [Edge observations](evidence/tab_edges.json)
- **Right.** Both near branches agree with the current pad normal within about 2.5° at the central intrinsics. The metal width is **4.74–4.84 mm**, major direction agrees with the CAD within about 3.3°, and independently outlined slot centers from the two images agree within **0.32–0.45 mm** at the assigned depth. The inner opening returns a circle-fit residual of 0.09–0.10 mm on the independent edge fit versus 0.13–0.25 mm on its steep branch, with unknown radius fitted freely. The rough pitch to the inner round is 25.8–26.1 mm on the near branches. Two off-rim metal descriptor matches failed to give a stable separate homography, and no off-plane shroud texture track survived on the right close pair. The independent relative shape check supports the 5 mm cutter width and existing approximate face direction. [Stereo branches and withheld rims](evidence/stereo_baseline.json) · [Independent round-hole fit](evidence/round_holdout.json)
- **Left.** Two tab-only fits differ materially. The earlier anchors give a roughly 3.8° seat discrepancy and 6.51/6.93 mm width in overview/close. New line anchors give 20.7° and 4.60/4.76 mm. I matched shell rim texture across the pair **with both holes masked**, found a coherent local shell patch and tested paired views under one camera motion. The near solution had about 2–3 px RMS shell epipolar error, a 34 px outlier on one tab anchor, and a roughly 17–24° CAD-normal discrepancy over focal and overview pose trials. The steep solution has larger metal residuals spread over several anchors and worse shell residuals. The independently traced near-branch outer rim centers can transfer to within **0.1–0.3 mm** even while the width and normal estimates disagree. The left fit currently supports a shallower family and leaves substantial seat-angle uncertainty. A 17–24° mismatch would create about **3–4.5 mm** of wedge across a 10 mm flat bearing footprint. The left inner hole is also a weak branch discriminator because the rear/lip boundary can move with view angle. [Left joint stereo fits and blocked shell holdouts](evidence/left_joint_stereo.json) · [Independent round-hole fit](evidence/round_holdout.json)

**Why depth is still unresolved.** The right close-up is essentially a local planar view. Holding the registered overview camera fixed, move all 3D tab points along rays by a common factor and move the close camera by the same similarity about the overview eye. Both rendered images, including the inner hole and pitch ratio, are invariant. The right test required only ±1.34% similarity to change the tab-plane location by ±5 mm and change transverse alignment about 3.7 mm. The left tab-only model shows the same image degeneracy. Off-plane shell texture supplies a left relative-motion constraint. The old shroud virtual corner that could provide metric scale on that side misses the observed close corner by at least 62 px in the baseline near joint fits even when baseline length is optimized. A steep branch can fit that single corner by driving the tab tens of millimeters into the surround. The corner is also 23 px away in the overview for the baseline case. This one metric bridge fails its two-view check. Old green points constrain the neighborhood of the inboard/upper left steel, but they do not directly measure the outer-slot plane. ## 3. Actual slots, stack and interference

Direct STEP measurement at both faces found square through-openings across **6.000 mm** of pad thickness. The right capsule is **7.600 × 5.000 mm**, the left **7.800 × 6.400 mm**. The signed centers agree with the cutter file within 0.001 mm. The right and left minimum inner-to-outer ligaments are **4.81 and 2.71 mm**. STL vertices on the slot boundary agree within 0.001 mm. The two formats represent one connected body and agree in volume to 0.07 mm³. - **Metal bearing and backing.** At central-depth close-image rectification, right notch clearance from slot center is about 6.1 mm. Left varies from roughly 5.4 to 6.5 mm across plausible edge/pose models. A centered Ø12 footprint is within a few tenths of the notch edge or overhangs it. An axis displaced 1 mm toward the notch consumes 1 mm of that clearance, so even the Ø10 result can become marginal. These are visible-face margins. The left photo shows paper and a wire through the outer opening and bundled wiring in the adjacent notch. The right shows a dark cavity with some reflected edge detail. Neither image shows the entire rear land or room to present a washer, capture a nut, or keep it away from the harness. ## Smallest check that resolves the decision

Dry-fit the current slotted part, if available, or a rigid transfer gauge located by the same hood seating features. Keep the shroud and harness installed. Use the **intended shank diameter** to test both openings simultaneously with no force. If a shank misses, retain the gauge in place and mark the top/bottom and left/right slot rim through the mount plane so the required signed offset is recorded. At each pin measure the gap at two opposite edges of the intended bearing footprint. This resolves the axial standoff and warns if a flat washer would bridge. On the left, check the green support edge for contact and verify that the actual backing and driver can be inserted with the harness at rest. Those checks target the quantities the two captures disagree on.

All files in this directory are review evidence. Reproduction uses the gauge-surround venv. The HEICs were converted with `sips -s format jpeg -s formatOptions best` to `evidence/raw_<number>.jpg`. Apply EXIF orientation with `magick -auto-orient` and rotate IMG_2850 an additional 90° clockwise. Save `evidence/u<number>.jpg`. Then run `discover.py`, `fast_discover.py`, `stereo_matches.py`, `manual_tracks.py`, `pose_check.py`, `tab_edges.py`, `tab_line_fit.py`, `stereo_fit.py`, `joint_stereo.py`, `left_bridge.py`, `round_holdout.py`, `reference_sensitivity.py`, `cad_measure.py`, `fit_stack.py`, `gauge_counterexample.py`, `shroud_check.py`, `collision_witness.py`. The masked descriptor sweeps can take several minutes. Relative shape fits use the prior independent outer-rim contours as held-out observations. The inner rims, new straight-edge profiles, texture matches, independent 3D checks and actual STEP/STL inspection were generated in this review.
