# Post-commit review of b36ab0f

Three independent reviewers checked the committed geometry, assembly and viewer, and restore process. The nominal corner closure passed. Physical test fitting is still needed, particularly at the outer ends of the gasket.

| Check | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Corner contact | Independent STEP rays matched both gasket faces to the chin and fiberglass at 4,473 car-right and 5,157 car-left sample points over the reviewed shoulder sections. A boundary intersection check between the rigid chin and the V9 fiberglass shell returned no section edges or vertices. |
| Fit sensitivity | At sampled gasket-tip positions, rigid-to-fiberglass distances were about 0.16 mm car-right and 0.21 mm car-left. These are local samples. The V9 cheek reconstruction has larger reported discrepancies against the scan, making the tips a priority for physical fit testing. |
| Edge detail | Each installed gasket ends with a small finite end face. The rigid rim has a rounded path in plan and a sharp cross-sectional edge at the seal land. Inspect possible hard contact on the fiberglass with the seal in place. |
| Assembly and viewer | The installed STEP reopened with 160 named leaves and four replacement components. The reviewer confirmed assembly signatures for both wings and the chin. Seal volume integration is numerically unstable on this export; the release audit includes a bidirectional sampled surface comparison with a maximum observed difference of 0.030 mm. The embedded GLB matched the archived file. Chrome loaded the offline model and both joint views. |
| Recovery | A clean Git archive restored all 17 archived files with matching sizes and SHA-256 digests. The closure generator reproduced the chin and gasket STLs byte for byte and matched the saved contact results. Both wing STLs regenerated byte for byte in a separate clean restore. Regenerated wing STEP DATA sections matched apart from the translator-generated product name on the right wing. |

The assembly is saved as a design and fit-test reference. Gasket free thickness, compression forces, moving tire clearance, road loads and aerodynamic performance have not been established.
