# Independent vehicle mount and LCD review

## Disposition

**Fail for a final surround with vehicle mount holes. Pass for the four OEM LCD mounting locations.** The released surround has four Ø3.40 mm LCD clearance axes and two solid vehicle mounting pads. The right and left outer slot targets are correctly identified, and the pad axes reproduce the source datums. Physical 3D registration, tab bearing, backing and installed access remain conditional. Both recommended vehicle openings below were cut together in memory from the released BRep. The result was one valid solid with no residual solid inside either opening. Only review artifacts were written.

## P1. Confirm the clamp stack and tool approach

- **Polymer side passes at the proposed sites.** Both Ø11 and Ø12 mm centered polymer bearing columns remained complete after each proposed cut. The cut extrusion and the extended axial envelopes clear the core, and the tool envelopes clear the core and the LCD reference. These are centered cylindrical screens. A fastener displaced by slot travel needs a fresh bearing check.
- **Metal side is unresolved.** The front-rim center is about 6.0–6.1 mm from the right notch edge and 5.4–5.6 mm from the left. A concentric Ø12 washer is tangent at best on the right and overhangs the left notch by about 0.4–0.6 mm on the nominal image plane, before allowing for edge radius or slot travel. A Ø10 footprint has roughly 1.0 and 0.4 mm notch margin at the center, with a smaller bearing area and unknown rear land. Any spacer or backing plate needs to seat on flat metal and support the metal around the aperture. The assembly supplies no vehicle attachment hardware.
- **Installed tool access is conditional.** At the proposed left center eight transformed scan samples enter the Ø12 × 25 mm driver path, the closest at 13.96 mm from its start. The tested Ø8 × 12 and Ø6 × 10 mm straight approaches miss the taped scan on both sides. Check against the installed shroud and harness. The sparse taped capture does not establish the full free volume, rear access, or clearance over column travel.
- **Actual face depth matters at both mounts.** The 7 mm right and 4 mm left gaps are allocations in the CAD. Under the package’s depth sensitivities alone, the left ranges from possible interference to roughly a 10 mm gap. Capture a common rigid hood datum, the two slot centers and two further surface depths per tab, then confirm flat land, metal thickness and what is behind each aperture. Use a rigid full-contact spacer or wedge if the measured geometry leaves a gap. A fit-check part with real openings can establish this before a loaded install. ## P2. The support path is continuous. Local ligament and root details still matter.

The finished STEP has a connected path from the right pad through its shoulder and rail to the surround. The left has a tapered rear rib as well as the shoulder and rail. Actual R2 cylinder faces 215 right and 2 left sit 0.10 and 0.76 mm from the former edge witnesses. The blends are local. The right retains approximately 15.1 and 11.4 mm sharp re-entrant edges at the two faces of the step about 27 mm above the opening. The left retains an 11.4 mm near-90° edge around (136.83, 193.96, −93.38), about 20 mm above the released axis, plus service-relief edges farther up. Witness coordinates and adjacency are in the evidence.

Through the proposed openings, the two remaining cross-section regions are **54.4 and 47.7 mm² right**, and **34.6 and 34.1 mm² left**. In the 10–22 mm band above the cut, the minimum weak-axis section modulus of the largest local connected slice of the **full** STEP is about 102 mm³ right and 147 mm³ left. The left full section includes the rail and rib where connected. The package’s lower support-only result measures a different section domain and sample placements. Neither is a load test. The left opening has the smaller edge ligament and weaker immediate cut section, and bearing overhang or a bridged stand-off can drive local bending or clamp slip. Keep the first cut to the proposed size. If physical fit requires shifting or widening it, recheck the left ligament and bearing patch before cutting further. A local left edge/neck thickening or concave blend is the narrowest follow-up if clearance permits. Retain the adjacent LCD nut service path. Polymer material, print orientation, heat creep and vibration capacity remain unverified. ## Pass. OEM LCD mounting pattern and seats

The unposed vendor REV1.3 STEP has four Ø3.50 mm cylindrical lug openings on a **173.700 × 98.400 mm** rectangle, with **0.600 mm** thick tabs and their back plane **4.200 mm** behind the front reference plane. The vendor drawing specifies the same pattern, opening diameter and tab thickness. The frame and glass solids in the exported assembly reproduce this geometry under a proper rigid transform. The assembly glass center and normal agree numerically with the old capture's corrected display reference to within 0.001 mm and 0.001°. These are CAD-to-CAD agreement figures.

The four **Ø3.40 mm** polymer axes are coaxial with the **Ø3.50 mm** metal lugs within 0.001 mm. Each facing seat matches its lug-back plane to within 0.001 mm and normal to within 0.001°. The actual planar lug-to-polymer contact intersection is **42.71 mm² per seat**; the full polymer seat faces range from 83.69 to 96.74 mm². The reference assembly contains the surround, LCD frame/glass and four sets of LCD hardware. These small holes are distinct from the vehicle mounting openings.
