2026-10-23 — research and proposed procedure only; no ECU or tune changes.

**Target:** 10 psi gauge = measured atmospheric pressure + 68.95 kPa. At 99 kPa baro, that is about 168 kPa absolute. Open-loop table entries are duty percentages, not pressure targets.

**Saved state inspected:** boost output Off; Open Loop; all 64 duty cells zero; pin 12; 34 Hz; boost-by-gear Off. Boost cut On at 120 kPa absolute, Spark Only, Full cut, protection gate 1500 RPM. ECU persistence is unverified. [CurrentTune](../CurrentTune.msq), [STATUS](../STATUS.md).

1. **Establish the valve/actuator baseline.** Trace the actual ECU driver and all three hoses. With regulated air, record actuator movement versus pressure and valve flow energized/deenergized; arrange loss of electrical power to deliver the base-boost configuration. Then log actual base boost with a direct pressure-source-to-actuator connection. Bench opening pressure alone does not establish running boost. The MAC catalog supports several plumbing arrangements, so do not assign port numbers from a generic internet diagram. [MAC 35 catalog, pp. 14–15](https://macvalves.com/wp-content/uploads/PDFs/Series_Pages/Sm3ways/35catalog.pdf).

2. **Verify output, then enable open-loop at zero duty.** Confirm pin 12 reaches a suitable protected solenoid driver, its current rating and suppression arrangement. Seller claims are 12 V/5.4 W; actual coil and driver remain unverified. The saved 34 Hz is a test candidate, not a manufacturer-verified specification for this installed valve. Check PWM/pressure response before adopting it. Firmware commands LOW at 0%, HIGH at 100%; physical boost direction depends on the driver and plumbing. **The saved 12% minimum/85% maximum apply to closed-loop; they do not constrain open-loop duty.** [Speeduino 202501.7 boostControl()](https://github.com/speeduino/speeduino/blob/202501.7/speeduino/auxiliaries.cpp).

3. **Set protection for each test stage.** Keep boost cut enabled and the 1500 gate; the integer RPM comparison actually permits protection from 1600 RPM. Choose each pressure ceiling just above the intended stage, using observed overshoot and sensor uncertainty, rather than immediately granting the full final ceiling. For final 10 psi commissioning, **178 kPa at 99 kPa baro** is a conditional candidate: about 11.46 psi, giving roughly 1.46 psi headroom. This is a proposed margin, not a proven safe limit; use it only if calibration supports that region and normal overshoot fits below it. Otherwise reduce duty/overshoot instead of widening the ceiling. The threshold is absolute and stored in 2 kPa increments. Retain Spark Only for this isolated control exercise; changing the shared protection cut mode also changes other limiters and warrants a separate decision. Check `engineProtectMAP`, not the legacy Boost Cut flag. [Speeduino 202501.7 protection implementation](https://github.com/speeduino/speeduino/blob/202501.7/speeduino/engineProtection.cpp), [local definition](../projectCfg/mainController.ini).

4. **Measure duty versus boost.** After the larger-injector/fuel and ignition work, compare zero-duty boost with the direct-hose baseline. In repeatable short load tests, increase the relevant RPM/TPS cells in small increments (initially 2 percentage points, the table's resolution), reviewing MAP, baro, RPM, TPS, duty, AFR, IAT and protection status each time. Establish intermediate levels before 10 psi; extend the tested RPM range progressively. Reduce duty where overshoot occurs. Do not assign a guessed “10 psi duty” or high-duty spool region. Recheck across gears and temperatures before calling the map repeatable. Closed-loop is optional later, using the measured duty map as its starting evidence.

5. **Save and verify each accepted stage.** Keep before/after files and the corresponding logs; burn, fully power-cycle and read back settings. The definition explicitly requires a power cycle for boost enable and frequency. Record actual peak/steady boost and the selected cut ceiling, not merely the commanded duty. [Local definition](../projectCfg/mainController.ini).

Outstanding measurements: actuator/base-boost behavior, physical port routing, driver identity/rating/polarity, installed valve identity and useful PWM range, measured baro, and repeatable transient overshoot. These determine the final settings.
