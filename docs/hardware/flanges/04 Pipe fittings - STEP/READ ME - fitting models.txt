ONSHAPE MOCKUP
Import the STEP models as solid parts. STEP files explicitly encode inches;
let the importer honor file units. Check an end diameter is about 48.26 mm.
Duplicate the tee and your selected elbow to build elbow-tee-tee-elbow.
Long and short elbows are alternatives, allowing comparison of packaging.
Use copies of the originals for cutting/splitting in your working model.
The tee branches and elbow head ends still require the oval shaping or
transition discussed for the Miata head flange. These files are unmodified
round fittings, not a completed manifold or a head-port-fit solution.

Before transferring cut marks to metal, match the actual purchased fitting's
radius, center-to-end length and end diameter to the model. Different vendor
blend shapes, tolerances and end bevels may differ. Models are for layout;
Onshape import has not been independently tested in this session.
